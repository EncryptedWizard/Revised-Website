Wheel Throwing

Learn how to shape a ball of clay with your hands using a pottery wheel. Classes are one hour and are available Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 1:00pm-2:00pm. Pricing starts as low as $100.

Sculpting

Learn how to sculpt clay using your hands and a range of tools we provide for you. Classes are 90 minutes and are available Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays from 2:00pm-3:00pm. Pricing starts as low as $100.

Pottery Painting

Pick out a hand-made piece and decorate it however you like! Most pieces range from $20 to $35. Kids 12 and under 20% off pottery painting all day on Saturdays.
